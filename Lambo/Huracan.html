Lamborghini Huracan STO

The Machine

Introduced in 2020, the Lamborghini Huracán STO (Super Trofeo Omologata) is a road-legal supercar inspired by Lamborghini’s Huracán Super Trofeo and GT3 race cars. Its naturally aspirated 5.2-litre V10 produces 640 CV (470 kW) and 565 Nm of torque, sending power to the rear wheels through a seven-speed dual-clutch transmission. It accelerates from 0–100 km/h in 3.0 seconds and reaches 310 km/h. Extensive carbon-fibre bodywork, a prominent rear wing and the distinctive one-piece front *cofango* reflect its racing-focused design. **Trivia:** The STO’s name means “Super Trofeo Homologated” in Italian, highlighting its connection to Lamborghini’s racing programme.
