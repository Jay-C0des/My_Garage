Ferrari 12Cilindri

The Machine

Introduced in 2024, the Ferrari 12Cilindri is a front-mid-engined, two-seat grand tourer that continues Ferrari’s long tradition of naturally aspirated V12 road cars. Its 6,496 cc (6.5-litre) V12 produces 830 cv (approximately 819 bhp) at 9,250 rpm and 678 Nm of torque at 7,250 rpm, sending power to the rear wheels through an eight-speed dual-clutch transmission. Ferrari claims a 0–100 km/h time of 2.9 seconds and a top speed exceeding 340 km/h. Designed by the Ferrari Styling Centre under Flavio Manzoni, its clean, geometric bodywork draws inspiration from the marque’s grand tourers of the 1950s and ’60s, while integrated active aerodynamics and modern chassis electronics bring contemporary performance technology. As the successor to the 812-era V12 grand-tourer tradition, the 12Cilindri makes a statement by preserving a high-revving, non-turbocharged twelve-cylinder engine in an increasingly electrified automotive landscape. Its design was recognised with the 2025 Car Design Award in the Production Cars category.

The Collector

The 12Cilindri looks like a modern version of the Daytona to me. It has the same iconic long bonnet and my favourite, coupe design. I really admire the paint job done on the model, it gives a metallic look to the car, which I believe suits it perfectly. In my opinion, the Ferrari branding is done the best on this model, out of all the Hot wheels Ferraris that I own. This model has a lot of tiny details done way better, like the side mirrors, which are absent in a lot of other models. There are some flaws though, as this thing has no headlights. Not even printed.
