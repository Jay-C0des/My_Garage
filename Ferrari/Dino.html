Ferrari Dino

The Machine

The Dino 246 GT was produced from 1969 to 1974 under Ferrari's Dino marque, named in honour of Enzo Ferrari's son, Alfredo “Dino” Ferrari. Its flowing, Pininfarina-designed body concealed a 2.4-litre, naturally aspirated 65-degree V6 mounted transversely behind the cabin, producing around 195 hp (143 kW) and driving the rear wheels through a five-speed manual gearbox. With a claimed top speed of 235 km/h, the lightweight 246 GT combined lively performance with balanced handling and elegant styling. Originally distinguished from Ferrari's V12 grand tourers by its smaller engine and separate Dino branding, it helped establish the appeal of mid-engined road-going sports cars and remains one of the most admired designs of the era.

The Collector

The Dino is regarded as one of the best looking cars, and I totally agree. The design is timeless. I really like its headlights and the wheels. The interior is silver, which looks odd to me, but none the less its well done. I have always found the "Dino" name to be quite fitting for this beautiful car. I would recommend this car to any and every Ferrari fan, as it distinguishes itself in the world of Ferrari's iconic models.
