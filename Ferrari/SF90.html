Ferrari SF90 Stradale

The Machine

Introduced in 2019, the Ferrari SF90 Stradale was Ferrari’s first series-production plug-in hybrid, named in honour of the 90th anniversary of Scuderia Ferrari. Its 4.0-litre twin-turbocharged V8 combines with three electric motors to deliver a total of 1,000 cv (986 bhp). An eight-speed dual-clutch transmission and electric front axle provide all-wheel drive, enabling a claimed 0–100 km/h time of 2.5 seconds and a top speed of 340 km/h. Advanced aerodynamics, including an active rear shut-off Gurney, help balance low drag with high-speed downforce. The SF90 marked a major turning point for Ferrari, blending its motorsport-derived engineering with plug-in hybrid technology in a road-going supercar.
