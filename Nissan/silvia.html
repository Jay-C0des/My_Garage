Nissan Silvia S15 Spec-R Aero

The Machine

Introduced in 1999, the **Nissan Silvia S15 Spec-R Aero** was the performance-oriented version of the final Silvia generation, produced until 2002. Its turbocharged 2.0-litre SR20DET four-cylinder engine delivered 250 PS (184 kW) and 275 Nm of torque, paired with a six-speed manual gearbox and rear-wheel drive. The Aero package added a prominent rear spoiler and sportier bodywork, while a helical limited-slip differential and revised suspension helped sharpen its handling. The S15 became an icon of Japanese drifting culture, celebrated for its lightweight chassis and tuning potential. The S15 was the last Silvia ever produced, bringing a 35-year lineage of Nissan's compact rear-wheel-drive coupes to an end.
