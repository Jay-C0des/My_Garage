Porsche 917

The Machine

Introduced in 1969, the **Porsche 917** was a purpose-built endurance racing prototype developed to compete in the World Sportscar Championship. Powered by an air-cooled, naturally aspirated 4.5-litre flat-12 engine producing around 520 PS (383 kW) in its original configuration, it sent power to the rear wheels through a five-speed manual gearbox. Its lightweight aluminium spaceframe and aerodynamic bodywork made it exceptionally fast, although early handling difficulties required significant development. The 917 secured Porsche's first overall victory at the 24 Hours of Le Mans in 1970, repeating the achievement in 1971. **Trivia:** The 917/30 Can-Am variant later developed over 1,100 PS in qualifying trim, becoming one of the most powerful racing cars of its era.
