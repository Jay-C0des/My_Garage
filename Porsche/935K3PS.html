Porsche 935 K3 Kremer Racing - Phillipe Salvet #41

The Machine

The **Porsche 935 K3**, developed by Kremer Racing in 1979, remains one of the greatest achievements of privateer motorsport engineering. Its twin-turbocharged flat-six and radically reworked aerodynamic body helped transform the 911-based racer into an endurance racing giant. Its defining moment came at the **1979 24 Hours of Le Mans**, where the #41 entry, driven by Klaus Ludwig, Don Whittington and Bill Whittington, secured an extraordinary overall victory against purpose-built prototypes. Conquering torrential rain and fierce competition, the K3 proved that independent engineering could challenge the might of factory racing teams. It remains an enduring symbol of Porsche's racing heritage and Kremer Racing's ingenuity.
