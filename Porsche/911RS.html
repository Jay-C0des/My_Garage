Porsche 911 Carrera RS 2.7

The Machine

Introduced in 1972, the **Porsche 911 Carrera RS 2.7** was developed as a homologation special for Group 4 motorsport, based on the original 911 (F-series). Its air-cooled, naturally aspirated 2.7-litre flat-six produced 210 PS (154 kW) and 255 Nm of torque, driving the rear wheels through a five-speed manual gearbox. Its lightweight construction, wider rear arches and distinctive ducktail spoiler improved both handling and high-speed stability. Designed to balance road usability with racing capability, the RS 2.7 became a landmark in Porsche's motorsport history. The ducktail was the first production rear spoiler fitted to a Porsche 911, helping reduce rear lift at high speeds. Only 1,580 examples were built, exceeding the original homologation requirement of 500.
