Porsche 911 Carrera S Cabriolet

The Machine

The **Porsche 911 Carrera S Cabriolet** is the open-top version of Porsche's iconic 911 sports car, combining everyday usability with traditional rear-engine performance. Depending on the generation, it features a flat-six engine, rear-wheel drive and a choice of manual or dual-clutch PDK transmission. In the 992-generation Carrera S (introduced in 2019), the twin-turbocharged 3.0-litre flat-six produces 450 PS (331 kW) and 530 Nm of torque, paired with an eight-speed PDK gearbox. Its electrically operated fabric roof preserves the familiar 911 silhouette while offering open-air driving. The Cabriolet's soft top can be opened or closed in approximately 12 seconds, even while travelling at speeds of up to 50 km/h.
