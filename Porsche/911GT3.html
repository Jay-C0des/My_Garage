Porsche 911 GT3 Cup

The Machine

The Porsche 911 GT3 Cup is a purpose-built customer racing car developed by Porsche for one-make motorsport championships. First introduced in 1998, the Cup series has evolved alongside successive generations of the 911, becoming one of the most successful customer racing programmes in motorsport. Depending on its generation, the car uses a naturally aspirated flat-six engine, rear-wheel drive and a competition-spec sequential or PDK transmission. Its stripped-out cockpit, large rear wing, racing slicks and aerodynamic bodywork distinguish it from the road-going 911 GT3. Built for close, competitive racing rather than road comfort, the GT3 Cup has become a familiar sight in Porsche Carrera Cup championships worldwide. Porsche's one-make Carrera Cup championships have helped develop generations of professional racing drivers, making the GT3 Cup an important stepping stone in international GT racing.
