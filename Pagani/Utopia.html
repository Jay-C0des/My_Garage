Pagani Utopia

The Machine

Introduced in 2022, the Pagani Utopia is the third major creation from Italian hypercar manufacturer Pagani, following the Zonda and Huayra. Its Mercedes-AMG-developed, 6.0-litre twin-turbocharged V12 produces 864 hp (635 kW) and 1,100 Nm of torque, delivered to the rear wheels through a seven-speed manual or automated manual transmission. Built around a lightweight carbon-titanium monocoque, the Utopia combines flowing bodywork with carefully integrated aerodynamics and an elegant interior featuring traditional analogue instruments. Despite its cutting-edge engineering, Pagani deliberately offered a genuine seven-speed manual gearbox, preserving the mechanical connection between driver and machine. The initial coupé production was limited to just 99 examples.
