Pagani Zonda Toon'd

The Machine

Introduced in 2009, the Pagani Zonda Cinque was created at the request of Pagani’s Hong Kong dealer as an exceptionally extreme, road-legal version of the Zonda, drawing inspiration from the track-only Zonda R. Its naturally aspirated 7.3-litre Mercedes-AMG V12 produces 678 CV (499 kW) and 780 Nm of torque, driving the rear wheels through a six-speed sequential gearbox. A carbon-titanium chassis, lightweight magnesium components and an adjustable rear wing help deliver extraordinary performance, including a 0–100 km/h time of 3.4 seconds. Only five coupés were built, followed by five roadsters, making the Cinque one of the rarest and most distinctive expressions of Pagani’s philosophy of combining art with engineering.
